Real data from Coherence Vault. Steps marked Coherence Vault open Coherence Vault, the MARQUIS database of published coherence data for superconducting qubits and resonators, where every number links to the page of the paper it came from. The 📚 lines under a module (Student depth and deeper) are computed live from it: the typical published value and its spread.
