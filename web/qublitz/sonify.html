🎵 Sonify Images

Turn an image's pixel rows into sound. Each row of the grayscale image is an envelope over time: brightness becomes amplitude. Multiplying it by a carrier at the modulation frequency turns that envelope into a tone you can hear, and its spectrum peaks at the carrier. Try the gallery, upload your own picture, or run a sound file the other way.
