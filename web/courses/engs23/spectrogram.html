Spectrogram Explorer

Load a recording (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg) and the page computes a short-time Fourier transform and draws magnitude in decibels on an inferno color scale, frequency versus time. Press play to hear it and a white playhead tracks the audio. You can also stream your microphone live and watch the spectrum scroll past. Everything runs locally in the browser and nothing is uploaded.

Source

The Recordings list has a bundled gong, four test signals generated in the page, and a few archive.org clips (Bach Goldberg is public-domain CC0; the 78 rpm items are from the Internet Archive Great 78 Project). A custom link needs a host that allows cross-origin reads, which archive.org does.
