Interactive Demos

Hands-on browser demos from our research and teaching, covering waves, Fourier modes, spectrograms, qubits and the cryogenics behind them. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is installed and nothing is uploaded.

Using these demos for outreach a suggested path, and how each activity works

Every activity opens with a short panel: what you will learn, how to play, and what counts as done. Many ask short questions along the way, inside the game, and each one ends with a 1-minute debrief: any remaining questions, with the answer and a short explanation shown right away, and two quick ratings: how fun it was and how much you learned. Work in pairs and say your prediction out loud before you press anything.

Quantum: learn a qubit by playing

  1. Qubit Copter: turn a qubit about x, y and z on the Bloch sphere.
  2. Rabi Rider: drive many runs toward |1⟩ while T₁ decay knocks them back.
  3. Beamline: a laser puzzle about pulse area and phase.
  4. QuBlitz Arena: a two-player duel where you measure at the right moment.
  5. Virtual Qubit Lab: finish by running your own pulse sequences on a simulated qubit.

Waves and sound: ENGS 23

  1. The Vibrating String: harmonics, a guitar pickup and distortion.
  2. The Drum Head: why a drum is not a string.
  3. Three Equations, One Eigenbasis: heat, waves and quantum on one set of modes.
  4. Spectrogram Explorer: read sound as a picture, with your own voice.

The ENGS 23 pages also have a guided version with predict-then-reveal steps: the Try the guided predictions link under each title.

Running a session? Add ?event=NAME to the link you hand out (letters, digits and hyphens), and the anonymous usage counts and answers from that session can be grouped together. No names or accounts are involved.
